Expanded Designer Bios:

Ava Thompson (Fairfield County, CT)
Ava specializes in blending traditional New England aesthetics with contemporary functionality.
Ava brings over 15 years of experience designing homes across Connecticut and the Northeast. With a background in architectural design and a passion for classical motifs, Ava crafts spaces that feel lived-in yet luxurious. Her signature projects often incorporate rich textures, elegant moldings, and custom millwork. Ava believes that every home should tell the story of its occupants, and she works closely with clients to understand their lifestyle and taste before laying out a vision.

Liam Rodriguez (Westchester County, NY)
Liam brings a modern minimalist approach to his designs, focusing on clean lines and open spaces.
A Westchester-based designer with a love for Scandinavian and industrial styles, Liam excels at decluttering and redesigning spaces with intention. His work has been featured in several regional design magazines. He believes in creating environments that are calm, balanced, and inspiring. Liam is also LEED-certified, making him the go-to expert for sustainable renovations and eco-conscious materials.

Sophia Chen (Long Island, NY)
Sophia's expertise lies in coastal-inspired interiors, incorporating natural textures and light-filled spaces.
Sophia merges her upbringing near the Long Island coast with a keen eye for serene, sunlit interiors. A master of blending form and function, she often works with natural linens, reclaimed wood, and glass accents to bring oceanic tranquility indoors. Clients praise her for her warm personality and ability to make large spaces feel cozy and intimate. She also specializes in outdoor-indoor flow and sunroom design.
